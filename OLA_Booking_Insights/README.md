# 🚖 Taxi Booking Data Insights #

This project explores booking trends and customer behavior using SQL Views on the bookings_1 dataset.
The queries uncover key business insights for ride-hailing platforms.

# 📊 Key Insights

✅ **Successful Bookings:** A dedicated view filters all rides where the booking status is "SUCCESS". This helps measure platform efficiency.

📏 **Average Ride Distance by Vehicle Type:**
Prime Sedans, Mini, and other categories show different average ride distances, providing insights into customer preferences and trip patterns.

🙋 **Cancelled Rides by Customers:**
A count of customer cancellations highlights service quality gaps or changes in customer intent.

🏆 **Top 5 Customers with Highest Number of Rides:**
Identifying loyal, high-frequency customers allows the business to design reward or loyalty programs.

🚗 **Driver Cancellations (Personal & Car Issues):**
Tracking such cancellations helps in operational improvements (e.g., vehicle maintenance, driver support).

⭐ **Driver Ratings (Prime Sedan):**
Minimum and maximum driver ratings for the Prime Sedan category reveal quality variation among drivers.

💳 **Payment Method (UPI):**
Filtering bookings made via UPI shows digital payment adoption trends.

🌟 **Average Customer Rating by Vehicle Type:**
Highlights which vehicle categories deliver the best customer experiences.

💰 **Total Booking Value (Successful Rides):**
Aggregated revenue from successful bookings gives a quick **business performance** snapshot.

❌ **Incomplete Rides with Reasons:**
Captures why rides were incomplete (e.g., traffic, driver/customer issues), guiding operational fixes.

# ✨ Why These Insights Matter?

These views transform raw booking records into actionable intelligence, enabling:

Better customer experience management 🚀

Stronger driver performance monitoring 🧑‍✈️

Smarter business strategy decisions 💼
